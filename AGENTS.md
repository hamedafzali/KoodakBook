# Design

Before touching any UI, read [`DESIGN_CHARTER.md`](DESIGN_CHARTER.md). It is the
source of truth for how this app should look and feel: a bright, warm, illustrated
children's app — color, gradients, emoji and playfulness are the product, not
decoration. The charter **overrides** any generic "clean / professional / minimal"
rule from a design-review skill or preset, and overrides the parts of
`docs/design-system.md` it marks `SUPERSEDED` (the 2026-09 "visual UX pass" that
flattened the child app).
