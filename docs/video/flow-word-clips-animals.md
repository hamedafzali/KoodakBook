# KoodakBook word clips — Animals (8 × 8 seconds)

One 8-second musical clip per word, made in Google Flow. On the free plan (50 credits/day) that is **5 words a day with Veo Lite** (10 credits each) or **2 a day with Fast** (20 each).

**Words:** اسب، خرگوش، سگ، فیل، ماهی، مرغ، گاو، گربه (the Animals lesson, in lesson order).

---

## 1. The series template (same for every word)

| Time | Picture | Sound (added in the editor) |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 s | The animal enters | Tune starts. First call of the word |
| 2–6 s | It does its fun action to the beat | Word two more times, on the beat |
| 6–8 s | It stops, faces the camera, smiles; hold | Word once more, slower. The written word appears |

**Chant pattern**, one word per beat at about 100 bpm: «اَسب! — اَسب! — اَسب! … اَسب.» Three quick calls on the beat, then one slower, clear call over the freeze.

**End card text** (added in the editor, not by Flow):
- The word in large letters, centred in the lower third, in the Vazirmatn font.
- Dark brown #3B2F2F on a cream #FFF3DE rounded box, from 6.0 s to the end.

---

## 2. Style block — paste at the start of EVERY prompt

> Cute cartoon animal in a soft, rounded children's picture-book style with simple shapes and thick soft outlines, pastel colours (cream, mint, peach, sky blue), sunny gentle lighting, plain uncluttered background. Static camera, centred subject, slow and smooth motion suitable for 3–7 year olds.

## 3. Sound block — paste at the END of every prompt

> Cheerful instrumental children's tune with ukulele, soft xylophone and light percussion, steady beat around 100 bpm. No speech, no singing, no voices, no on-screen text, no letters.

**Optional:** to make the series look like KoodakBook, upload one style frame and use it as an **Ingredient** in every clip. Good choices are a screenshot of the app's Home hills scene or one story illustration.

---

### 3b. Singing version of the sound block (not recommended)

**Tested 2026-10-26:** Veo sings Persian with a Dari/Afghan accent, and prompts don't fix it. Use the instrumental block in section 3 and record the voice yourself (section 3c).

Use this when you want Flow to sing the word itself. Replace `WORD` with the Latin spelling, `PERSIAN` with the Persian spelling and `MEANING` with the English word, e.g. `asb`, `اسب` and `horse`.

> Cheerful children's song with ukulele, soft xylophone and light percussion, steady beat around 100 bpm. A warm, happy child's voice sings in Persian (Farsi), with a native Persian (Iranian) accent, only one Persian word, "WORD" (PERSIAN, the Persian word for MEANING): three short calls on the beat — "WORD! WORD! WORD!" — then one slow, clear "WOOORD" at the end while the animal smiles. Clear pronunciation, no other words, no English lyrics, no on-screen text, no letters.

**Check the pronunciation in every take.** AI singing in Persian can come out wrong, e.g. "asb" as "asp". If it's off:
- regenerate once;
- if it's still off, mute the voice in CapCut and record the word yourself over Flow's tune.

Never publish a mispronounced word: the whole point is that children learn the right sound.

### 3c. Poem version (from clip 4 on)

A two-line Persian rhyme, with the word on its own at the end. Flow makes the clip with instrumental music only (section 3); the rhyme is recorded by a real voice and added in CapCut. Keep it to two short lines, since 8 seconds can't hold more.

| # | Rhyme (Persian) | Latin for the prompt |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | فیل اومده، فیل اومده / با خرطومِ بلند اومده — فیل! | feel oomadeh, feel oomadeh / baa khar-toom-e boland oomadeh — feeel! |
| 5 | ماهی، ماهی، توی آب / حباب می‌سازه، حباب حباب — ماهی! | maahi, maahi, tooye aab / habaab misaazeh, habaab habaab — maahi! |

## 4. The 8 prompts

Each prompt is: **Style block + the scene below + Sound block.**

### 1 · اسب — horse
- **Say:** اَسب · *asb*
- **Scene:** A friendly cartoon horse with a fluffy mane trots in from the left across a green meadow, then prances in place three times to the beat, mane bouncing, then stops, turns to face the camera and smiles. Clip ends on the smile.

### 2 · خرگوش — rabbit
- **Say:** خَرگوش · *khargush*
- **Scene:** A small white cartoon rabbit with long ears hops in from the right on soft grass, does three big bunny hops in place to the beat with ears flopping, then sits still, wiggles its nose and smiles at the camera. Clip ends on the smile.

### 3 · سگ — dog
- **Say:** سَگ · *sag*
- **Scene:** A happy brown cartoon puppy runs in from the left in a sunny garden, wags its tail and spins around once to the beat, then sits down neatly, tilts its head and smiles at the camera. Clip ends on the smile.

### 4 · فیل — elephant
- **Say:** فیل · *fil*
- **Scene:** A round, soft grey baby elephant walks in slowly by a small pond, lifts its trunk and sprays a gentle fountain of water three times to the beat, sparkles in the air, then lowers its trunk and smiles at the camera. Clip ends on the smile.

### 5 · ماهی — fish
- **Say:** ماهی · *mâhi*
- **Scene:** A bright orange cartoon fish swims in from the right in clear light-blue water with soft bubbles, swirls in a little loop and blows three bubbles to the beat, then floats still, facing the camera, and smiles. Clip ends on the smile.

### 6 · مرغ — hen
- **Say:** مُرغ · *morgh*
- **Scene:** A plump white cartoon hen with a small red comb waddles in from the left in a farmyard, flaps its wings three times to the beat with a few soft feathers floating, then settles down and smiles at the camera. Clip ends on the smile.

### 7 · گاو — cow
- **Say:** گاو · *gâv*
- **Scene:** A gentle black-and-white cartoon cow stands in a green field, swishes its tail and nods its head three times to the beat, a small bell on its neck swinging, then looks straight at the camera and smiles. Clip ends on the smile.

### 8 · گربه — cat
- **Say:** گُربه · *gorbe*
- **Scene:** A fluffy grey cartoon kitten chases a small red yarn ball rolling in from the left, pats it three times to the beat, then sits up, curls its tail and smiles at the camera. Clip ends on the smile.

**Note:** the app's cast includes تندپا, a rabbit, and پشمک, a cat. For clips 2 and 8 you can upload their pictures as Ingredients so the series uses KoodakBook's own characters. This needs Veo Fast or Quality; Lite doesn't take reference images.

---

## 5. Settings in Flow

- **Mode:** Text to video. Use Ingredients to video only when you add a style frame or character.
- **Model:** Veo Lite for all eight. Redo only your favourite one or two with Fast.
- **Length:** 8 s. Aspect ratio: **9:16** for Telegram/phone, or 16:9 for YouTube/landing. Pick one and keep it for the whole series.
- Generate 1–2 takes per word, and keep the take where the animal ends cleanly facing the camera.
- **If the clip has voices or text anyway:** regenerate, or mute the clip's audio and add your own music in the editor.

---

## 6. Finishing in CapCut (free) — about 5 minutes per clip

1. Import the clip. Keep Flow's music, or mute it and add one tune for the whole series.
2. **Record the word**, preferably a real voice saying or singing it. Place the calls at about 0.8 s, 2.6 s, 4.4 s, and the slow one at 6.3 s, on the beats.
3. **Add the written word** at 6.0–8.0 s: Vazirmatn font, dark brown on a cream box, as in section 1.
4. **Export** at 1080p and 30 fps. Name the files `01-asb.mp4` … `08-gorbeh.mp4` (same base names as the raw files).
   Save them in `KoodakBook/releases/media/word-clips/animals/`. Next to each finished clip, keep Flow's original as `01-asb-raw.mp4`. Git ignores `releases/`, so the video files never end up in a commit.
5. **Optional:** join all eight into one ~70-second "Animals song" video. Put a 1-second title card «حیوانات» at the start and a recap at the end, where all eight words flash by once more.

## 8. File names

Save every Flow download into `releases/media/word-clips/animals/` and keep Flow's name. Then run:

```
python3 releases/media/word-clips/rename-clips.py
```

It renames each clip from the animal in its name: `01-asb-raw.mp4`, `02-khargoosh-raw.mp4`, `03-sag-raw.mp4`, `04-feel-raw.mp4`, `05-maahi-raw.mp4`, `06-morgh-raw.mp4`, `07-gaav-raw.mp4`, `08-gorbeh-raw.mp4`. A second take becomes `…-raw-2.mp4`. Files already numbered are left alone. The edited CapCut export drops `-raw`, e.g. `02-khargoosh.mp4`.
